I'm Jorge Alejandro Delarbre López, a communications strategist with over 14 years building corporate reputation, managing crisis, and driving brand visibility across Latin America. My career spans agency and in-house roles, from leading communications for global multinationals to building entire communications functions from scratch in some of the region's most demanding environments.

At JeffreyGroup, one of Latin America's leading corporate communications agencies, I was Director of Corporate Communications, advising accounts including Nestlé México, American Airlines, PepsiCo, Rappi, Aon and ROADIS. Before that, at LLYC, I managed strategic communications for Walmart, Autlán, Carvajal, Marathon Petroleum and Volvo Trucks across crisis, media and stakeholder fronts.

My regional scope expanded at vivo, where as Senior PR Manager for Latin America I led communications across Colombia, Mexico, Panama, Peru and Chile, serving as official regional spokesperson and managing HQ alignment. I led product launches and brand expansion across the region, including vivo's entry into Panama.

I currently serve as Director of Communications at Tulum Country Club and PGA Riviera Maya, where I professionalized the communications infrastructure during a transition to an in-house, federated model: brand narrative, annual planning, competitive benchmarking, spokesperson strategy and media relations, plus a governance model separating editorial, operational and commercial content flows. As a sole communications professional, I also led communications for a major international golf tour event, with pre-tournament results that nearly tripled the prior year's full output.

What sets my approach apart is the mix of Communication Sciences and Psychology, both from UNAM, plus a Master's in Communication. That formation lets me read not just what a narrative says, but what it produces in audiences, decision-makers and organizational culture.

TealKin is my personal project for sharing the thinking, tools and perspectives built over more than a decade at the intersection of corporate reputation, crisis management and strategic communications.
